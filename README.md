# Cel Blade

Cel-shaded arcane-tech action MMORPG — Three.js, plain ES modules, no build step for the client;
a Node server runs the shared world.

```
npm install               # once (three, the WebSocket library and the Postgres driver, for the server)
npm start                 # game server: serves the client + the online realm at http://localhost:8080
ADMINS=youraccount npm start   # the same, with your account as an admin
npm test                  # smoke test: sign up, join the world, chat
python serve.py 5173      # or: offline-only dev server (single-player), http://localhost:5173
```

How online play works (the shared world, combat netcode, parties, trade, guilds, mail) is in
[docs/MULTIPLAYER.md](docs/MULTIPLAYER.md). Running a realm (one process, Docker with TLS, or a Kubernetes cluster of gateways and layers that grows to thousands of players; settings, admins,
backups, monitoring, capacity) is in [docs/DEPLOY.md](docs/DEPLOY.md).

## Controls
| | |
|---|---|
| Move / look | WASD · mouse (click to lock) |
| Traversal | Shift hold sprint / tap dash (air dash in the air) · Space jump (hold for full height, again to double jump) · hold C to slide (slopes speed you up) · Space from a slide = bullet jump · hold RMB in the air to hover-glide (keeps your speed) · walk or jump into ledges to clamber |
| Combat | LMB attack / fire / hold to charge (magic) · RMB heavy (melee, on the ground) / aim down sights (ranged & magic) · LMB in the air while looking down = ground slam · Q quick melee · R reload · 1-3 swap weapon |
| World | E interact (talk, rest, attune waystones, open chests) · aim the reticle at loot: F picks it up, hold F swaps it for the weapon you're wielding (or the armour in its slot) · I inventory (hover for details; drag items between slots; drag out to drop, onto the trader's stock to sell) · M world map |
| View | V toggles first / third person |
| Menus | Esc opens the game menu (and backs out of it, or a dialogue) · P character · I / Tab inventory · L journal · M map · O social · G guild · Q / E switch tabs (Social, Guild and Mail are online only). The world keeps running while a menu is open |
| Chat (online) | Enter world chat · `/s` nearby · `/w Name` whisper · `/r` reply · `/p` party · `/g` guild · `/who` |
| Social (online) | `/invite Name` party (Y / N to answer) · `/leave` · `/trade Name` · `/friend Name` · `/ignore Name` · `/guild create Name TAG` · `/report Name reason` · admins: `/admin …` ([list](docs/DEPLOY.md#admins)) |
| Touch (phones, tablets) | Played sideways. Left thumb anywhere on the left moves (the stick follows it; hold it right out to sprint) · drag on the right to look · Fire / Aim (keep dragging the same thumb to aim), Jump, Dash, Slide (tap on, tap off — slide then Jump to bullet jump), Reload and Melee buttons · a left-hand Fire and Aim (tap on, tap off) to shoot with the left thumb while the right aims · tap the interaction prompt, the loot card (hold to swap) or a weapon slot · Menu, Chat and View at the top · pinch and drag the map. Settings › Touch controls (Auto / On / Off), touch look speed. Add it to the home screen to play fullscreen |
| Dev | `` ` `` or F1 dev panel · F3 performance overlay |

Momentum carries: bullet jumps launch faster than the slide that fed them (chains build speed up to ~36 m/s), glides only slow your fall, air steering bends momentum instead of braking it, and air dashes add to whatever speed you have.

## Systems at a glance
- **Characters & animation** (`models/Characters.js`, `models/Human.js`, `anim/`): every character is a bone skeleton (hips → spine → chest → head, two-segment arms and legs with hands and feet) baked into skinned meshes (`ModelKit.bakeSkinned`: one mesh per colour, one ink shell per thickness, indexed so the skinning shader transforms each shared vertex once). Humans are built at real proportions — about seven heads tall, legs half the height — as one continuous lofted skin per region (torso and neck, each arm, each leg; `render/Loft.js`) with blended weights, so waist, ribs, neck, elbows, knees and hips bend smoothly; muscle shape (deltoids, biceps, forearms, glutes, quads, kneecaps, calves) is in the cross-sections, and clothes are bands of the same lofts, so they bend with the body. Hands are jointed fists — four three-segment fingers and a thumb that wrap the actual handle of whatever is held, or hang relaxed. Heads are sculpted: a cranium, a face lofted chin to brow (jaw, cheekbones), nose, brow, lips, ears, set-in eyes with lash lines and brows, hair caps to a real hairline (short spiky, long with locks, bun, bald) and full beards. `Animator.js` is fully procedural: a gait with planted feet (two-bone leg IK onto terrain), hip bob/sway, lean into acceleration, bank into turns, landing compression, flips, slides, dashes (a low lunge along the dash, weapon held steady), mantles and deaths, all blended through springs. Weapons are held weapon-first (`WeaponMotion.js`): carries per weight class, guns placed against the body with both hands solved by IK, and melee swings that travel arcs around the torso. Weight shapes every swing: heavy weapons hang at the cocked point, keep accelerating through the hit, trail the hands then whip through, cut on a diagonal, and drag the body into a crouched, wide-stanced follow-through before heaving back; the whirlwind coils, builds speed with the blade at arm's length and carries past a full turn.
- **Guns** (`combat/Projectiles.js`): ballistic rounds at real muzzle velocities (360–920 m/s) with drop, swept ray hits (no tunnelling), thin tracers (every third round bright), surface-specific impacts with bullet holes, ejected casings and camera recoil. Spread is screen-space: the reticle keeps its size and aiming down sights tightens the cone through the zoom.
- **Holding weapons** (`anim/WeaponMotion.js`): pistols are one-handed; long guns rest at low ready (stock seated, muzzle dipped, sights up), seat the stock in the shoulder to aim (torso bladed, support hand on a real foregrip); launchers ride the shoulder. Blades, hafts and staves run *through* the fist, thumb toward the head, and each hand turns about the handle so its wrist faces the arm (`Animator.js` `handleFrame`). Melee weapons are gripped low on the handle and swing on arcs split between torso turn and arm sweep, so blades and pommels stay clear of the body; windups travel along the arc.
- **Bodies, armour and capes** (`render/Shell.js`, `models/`): chest armour is lofted from the body's own torso (`Human.BODY`, superellipse shells with pecs and shoulder blades), so plates hug it; helms are fitted to the head and leg armour stretched to the legs (`ArmorForms.applyArmor`). Five armour slots — head, chest, hands, legs and **back**: capes are skinned cloth on a grid of five bone chains simulated by `anim/Cloth.js` (gravity, air drag and internal damping, flutter waves, folding across the width, bending, collision with the back's real curve, seat, legs and shoulder line), so they ripple side to side as well as swing.
- **First person** (V; `render/CameraRig.js`, `render/ViewModel.js`): the camera rides a steady eye height that follows how low the body is carried (crouches, slides, landing dips and climbs, all eased) but never the head's own motion — flips, corkscrews, lunges and gait bob don't move the view; the body is drawn shadow-only and faces where you look. The held weapon is a viewmodel with the body's own jointed hands (fingers round the handle, the pistol grip or the foregrip; the free hand relaxed) and bare forearms in the character's skin tone — both hands on two-handed weapons and gun foregrips — in a hold per type (daggers forward, swords and axes up at the right, greatswords and hammers across the body, spears couched, staves upright). Melee swings follow each attack's path and timing: an anticipation pull, an accelerating strike that crosses the view, a follow-through, a blade trail, a camera roll into the stroke and a jolt on impact. Guns raise their real sights to the screen centre when aiming — a notched rear sight and glowing front post, a holo sight whose dot is collimated (it always marks where the gun points), or a full scope view with extra zoom — and aimed shots follow the sight line, so they land on the dot even as recoil and sway move it. Aimed recoil is mostly a push straight back, so automatic fire keeps the sight in view. Every sight is styled by maker (`items/WeaponModel.js`): scopes on the model and in the scope view (`ui/ScopeView.js`) — Kessler military mil-dots with a rangefinder, Rattlecap scratched brass with a cracked lens, Voltaic digital chevron with a live readout and scanlines, Rime crystal with a frosted rim; holo sights — Kessler's closed box hood with a ring-and-ticks reticle, Rattlecap's lens wired into a brass ring with a painted cross, Voltaic's white loop with a glowing chevron, Rime's hexagonal crystal frame with a diamond; iron sights — Kessler's peep and winged post, Rattlecap's bent-plate V-notch and bolt post, Voltaic's glowing three-dot sights, Rime's crystal-shard notch and ice-spike post (raised on a riser wherever the receiver would block the line). Crossbows carry theirs above the bolt; launchers on a bracket along the tube's left side, so aiming puts the tube on your right shoulder. Ink outlines on the first-person weapon and hands are drawn on their own thinner layer, which thins further as you aim, so they never swallow a sight. Also walk bob, look sway, reload, sprint and swap poses; shots, muzzle flash and casings come from the viewmodel's gun.
- **Hitstop**: a landed blow freezes only the two fighters for a beat; the rest of the world (effects, other enemies, shots) keeps moving. Loot from a kill is built a few frames later so it never lands on the killing blow's frame.
- **Codex** (tome): burns a rune where you aim that detonates a moment later; charged casts ripple five runes down the aim line.
- **Graphics quality** (Settings › Graphics quality, `render/Quality.js`; phones start on Low, everything else on High):
  - *Screen-space ink* (`render/PostFX.js`): outlines drawn from a depth and normal pre-pass — silhouettes and creases sharper than ~60° on everything, fading with the fog. They replace the world's inverted-hull outlines; the first-person weapon keeps its own thinner hull ink and is masked out.
  - *Bloom*: glowing things (every unlit material: energy, crystals, runes, shots, glowing blades) are lifted past white so only they bloom, then brought back into range keeping their hue.
  - *Ambient occlusion* (High): from the same pre-pass, at half resolution — creases, contact under feet, rocks set into the ground.
  - *Cascaded shadows* (High, `render/Shadows.js`): two cascades out to 150 m, so distant trees and buildings cast shadows. Lit materials are set up the first time they appear in the scene, and put back when cascades are switched off.
  - *Grass* (Medium and High, `render/Grass.js`): a field of up to 70,000 blades round the player in one instanced draw, fixed in the world (it doesn't swim as you walk), coloured like the ground beneath and thinning on roads, rock, sand, snow and dirt yards; it bends with the wind and away from your feet.
  - *Wind* (`render/Wind.js`, every quality): trees, bushes, reeds, grass tufts and flowers sway downwind, crowns more than trunks, with gusts rolling across the land — their outlines, shadows and ink sway with them.
  - *Speed FX*: zoom blur toward your direction of travel plus comic speed lines, scaled by speed (off when slow).
  - On Low the scene is drawn straight to the screen with hull ink, going through the post chain only for the speed effect.
- **Endless world** (`src/world/`): generated from the save's seed (`Gen.js`) — continental noise, mountain ranges, rivers and lakes, and climate-driven biomes. The land is cut into named Voronoi regions (~240 m), each owned by a race and levelled by its distance from the hub, with its own waystone, camps (and at higher levels a stronghold with a boss), towers, ruins, mesas, chests and roads linking the waystones. `Chunks.js` streams 64 m terrain chunks around the player with three LODs; `World.js` streams points of interest, roads, instanced scenery and colliders in and out with them, within a per-frame time budget.
- **Enemy races** (`combat/bestiary.js`, `combat/races.js`, `models/Creatures.js`): four races, each with its own mob types, fighting styles and level-gated sub-types —
  - *Goblin warband*: darting cutters (scrappers), shieldbashers whose guard blocks frontal hits (ironclads), sappers lobbing fused bombs (firebombers), shamans who ward and mend their allies (spark shamans), hired ogre bruisers, and the Ogre Warlord.
  - *The Hollow*: shambling husks (bloated husks burst when they die), bone archers (marksmen), wraiths that float, blink away and cast homing void bolts (frost shades), shield-and-sword revenants (captains), and the Hollow King.
  - *Arcane constructs*: sentry drones that orbit overhead and fire bursts (arc sentries), blade-armed striders that charge, mortar-backed bulwarks (siege), and the Colossus.
  - *Wild beasts*: wolf packs that circle and dart in (frostfangs, alphas whose howl frenzies the pack), web-spitting, pouncing spiders that lie in ambush (venom), charging tuskers (ironhides), and the Elder Ursa.
  Bipeds share the humanoid animator; beasts and spiders run on `anim/LegAnimator.js` (per-leg gait cycles with IK foot planting, body riding the terrain, bite/charge/pounce/rear/howl poses) and drones and wraiths on its hover rig. Every attack is data (`Enemy.js` runs swing, combo, bash, bite, charge, pounce, arrow, bolt, burst, fan, lob, stomp, nova, ward, heal, howl and summon); enemy hits carry their element's status.
- **Encounters**: camps spawn near the player, individual enemies respawn after a delay, roaming packs belong to the race whose land they cross and scale with its level, elites are named and tougher, bosses call reinforcements as they weaken. Resting at the Hearth Reactor resets camps and chests.
- **Map & discovery**: fog of war (`Discovery.js`, sparse per-chunk cells), full map (`ui/MapUI.js`) with region names, race colours, custom markers and waystone fast travel; minimap and compass show quest and custom markers. Contracts and hunts are generated from nearby camps and races.
- **Audio** (`audio/`): all procedural Web Audio — guns voiced per type (crack, body, tail, action) and tinted by element and manufacturer, reloads timed to the animation, weighted melee whooshes and hits, spell casts, explosions, footsteps by surface, movement sounds, wind/slide/glide beds, and enemy calls (howls, roars, casts, bowstrings, shield clangs, fuses). Volume and mute live in Settings.
- **Loot**: weapons (18 types) and armour (5 slots) are generated from recipes — type → rarity → manufacturer → stat parts → visual component forms → perks. Each manufacturer has a signature energy colour, emblem and its own subset of component shapes (`items/forms.js`, `models/ArmorForms.js`). Saves store only recipes.
- **Weapon names** (`items/names.js`): the base name is a model line taken from the frame the weapon is built around — a gun's receiver, a blade's hilt, a haft, a shaft, a launcher tube, a crossbow's prod, a codex cover, an orb shell — plus its type, so guns on the same receiver share a line ("Talon Rifle", "Talon Pistol"). One supplementing word comes from what's fitted: the elemental core if there is one ("Caustic Talon Rifle"), otherwise the most notable attachment ("Sniping Hauler Rifle"). Legendaries are named only after their legendary power and class ("Emberfang", "The Ravenous Verdict", "Volt's Verdict"), with no model line or supplement.
- **RPG**: attributes, levels, gear stats, quests (`rpg/Quests.js`), trader, backpack, loot filter.
- **Title screen** (`ui/TitleMenu.js`): the logo and menu over a slow flight round your character — Continue (with the save's level, region and gold), New Game (confirmed before it replaces a save), Settings and Controls; arrow keys or W/S and Enter work too.
- **Game menu & settings** (`ui/GameMenu.js`, `core/Prefs.js`): a full-screen hub with tabs — Character (level, attributes, combat stats, equipped gear, loot filter), Inventory (`ui/InventoryUI.js`; also the trader's shop), Journal, Map (`ui/MapUI.js`), and online Social (party, players nearby, friends), Guild and Mail (`ui/SocialUI.js`), then Settings and Controls. It opens on Esc or whenever play loses the mouse, and it doesn't stop the world: the character stands idle (and can still be hit — the header shows live health). Only single-player freezes, and only while the window is in the background. Settings apply live, are stored per device rather than in the save, and are reachable from the title screen. Browsers only hand the mouse back after a click, so returning to play by keyboard shows a "click to resume" prompt.
- **Phones and tablets** (`ui/TouchControls.js`, `core/Device.js`): touch controls drive the same input state as keyboard and mouse (`core/Input.js`): a floating stick, drag-to-look, an action cluster under the right thumb, and the HUD's own prompts as buttons. The HUD rearranges round the thumbs, menus scale down on short screens, and portrait asks to be turned. Phones start on lighter graphics (no multisampling, shadows, hatching or speed effects; 75% resolution), and adaptive resolution steps the resolution down while frames run slow. A web app manifest lets it be installed and run fullscreen in landscape.
- **Online** (`server/`, `net/`, `sim/`, `ui/TitleMenu.js`): when the page comes from the game server, the title signs you in (accounts, up to six characters each, a look picker) and enters the shared realm. The server runs the world's enemies (`sim/`, the same code offline single-player runs locally) and owns every character (`rpg/CharacterCore.js`): the client predicts and the server confirms. Other players appear fully animated in their own gear. Kills share XP and gold with everyone who fought, and each player gets their own loot roll. Parties of four (`ui/PartyUI.js`), a two-sided trade window (`ui/TradeUI.js`), friends, guilds with tags and chat, and mail with attachments (`ui/SocialUI.js`); world, nearby, whisper, party and guild chat (`ui/ChatUI.js`). In the cluster the realm runs as layers (copies of the world, ~110 players each) behind gateways (`server/gateway.js`), sharing Postgres; chat, friends, guilds, mail and admin reach across them and parties are brought together on one. See [docs/MULTIPLAYER.md](docs/MULTIPLAYER.md).
- **HUD** (`ui/HUD.js`, `style.css`): frontier sci-fi interface — chamfered dark-glass panels with hairline edges, energy-teal and gilt accents, diamond markers and tracked condensed capitals (Saira Condensed + Chakra Petch). Compass strip, slanted segmented vitals with a hexagonal level badge, weapon card with magazine pips, damage-direction arcs, low-health vignette.
- **Ink**: on Low (and on the first-person weapon at every quality) outlines are inverted hulls; their width is set per layer (`INK_SCALE` in `render/Toon.js`). World outlines thin as the view zooms in (aiming, scopes) so a magnified target isn't buried in ink, and the first-person weapon has its own thinner layer that thins again while aiming.

## Dev tools
- In game: `` ` `` / F1 opens the dev panel — god mode, infinite resources, noclip, one-hit kills, levels and gold; teleport to any camp / waystone / landmark or the reticle; spawn any enemy or sub-type (level, count, elite) at the reticle; an audio board that plays every sound; forge any weapon type or armour slot at any rarity and level; render toggles (outlines, hatching, fog, shadows, speed FX, player skeleton, world colliders); time scale and pause; save export/wipe; a JS console (`g`/`game`, `p`, `THREE` in scope); and the error log. F3 toggles the performance overlay. The game loop never freezes on an exception — errors are caught, logged there and badged on screen.
- `tools/armory.html` — grid of generated items, filter by type / manufacturer.
- `tools/figure.html` — characters up close at any graphics quality: looks lined up, heads, hands blown up 8×, test poses for joint deformation, random armour, NPCs and creatures.
- `tools/shotkit.js` — scripted-test helpers (pinned clock, frame stepping, key taps, portrait captures and contact sheets).
- `serve.py` (and the game server in development) also accepts `POST /__shot/<name>` (a PNG data URL) and writes it to `.shots/`.
- `node tools/bot.mjs [Name] [server] [count] [spread]` — test players that sign in to a running server and jog round the hub, firing the odd burst (throwaway local accounts). With a spread (metres) each jogs somewhere of its own within that distance: a load test ([docs/DEPLOY.md](docs/DEPLOY.md#capacity)).
