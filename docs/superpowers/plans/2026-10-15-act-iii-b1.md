## Decisions (defaults; flag them in the review)
1. **The woken waystone** (`ironstone`): the nearest waystone to Ironreach outside its walls. It's an ordinary waystone, as Ysolde's first stone was. c2 is fought there and c4 speaks there.
2. **c4 "use"** is a touch of the stone, as p3's is (`goto` with `r: 4`, then the `emissary` moment), not a `use` object, because waystones aren't quest objects. The beat's "talk" is the report back to Isa.
3. **c2's Synth scouts** come as a `defend` at the stone (`race: 'synth'`, 2 waves). The region's own race isn't always Synth, and a defend brings its own waves.
4. **c3's settlement** (`ironhamlet`): the nearest hamlet, village or town to Ironreach that has a war-walker road (`roadRoute`), within 1400 m.
5. **The plea** is a moment (`plea`) at the Brightwater waystone after Ysolde's talk. The Pilgrim speaks where it first spoke.
6. **Journal:** ACTS gains Act III (16–24). Until Act III is complete, the "story so far is told" line says Act III continues in a later update.
7. **Dressing:** Ironreach's wall towers carry foundry smokestacks (as Farcall's carry masts), and Isa stands by the citadel door, where Vey stands in Farcall.
