# Act III, B-2: the Fallen Titan and quests c6–c7 — Implementation Plan

> Executed inline, as B-1 was. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-act-iii-story-design.md` §3, beats 6–7. Task: WORK_PLAN B-2.

**Goal:** the Fallen Titan becomes a story place near Ironreach. Quest c6 drives Synth salvagers off it and pulls its control core (`titan_core_taken`). Quest c7 wakes the core at Isa's yard while Synths home in, and it shows an earlier intrusion.

## Places (world/story.js; appended to ORDER so nothing earlier moves)
- **`titan`**: *The Fallen Titan*, out from Ironreach (`from: 'city3'`). A `landmark` of kind `titan`, so the existing kneeling-Titan builder draws it, with a quest object `titan:core` (new model `titancore`: the core glowing in its chest housing until it's pulled).
- **`salvage`**: *Salvage Rig*, a Synth `relay` camp about 40 m from the Titan: the salvagers c6 drives off (a `clear` step).
- **`yard`**: *Brannock's Yard*, Isa's salvage yard just outside Ironreach's walls (new STORY_BUILDERS `yard`: a fenced yard, a gutted Synth hulk, a gantry crane, slag). Its quest object is `yard:cradle` (new model `cradle`: the core resting cold until it's woken). Its open middle is the ground c7 holds.
- `storyPlaces` gains the `city3` anchor.
- **Generator titans:** the name pool's "Fallen" becomes "Shattered", so no procedural landmark is ever called "The Fallen Titan". The list keeps its length, so only titans that were "Fallen" are renamed.

## Quests (lines are proposals)
- **c6 The Fallen Titan** (19, Isa): reach the Titan (moment `titan`), clear the Salvage Rig, pull the core. Sets `titan_core_taken`.
- **c7 Reading the Core** (20, Isa): wake the core in the yard's cradle, then hold the yard against 3 Synth waves (moment `coreread`). The log reads CONTAINMENT HELD, ORIGIN UNKNOWN. Who it was stays open for Act IV (DEC-1c).
