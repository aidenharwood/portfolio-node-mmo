# Act III, B-3: the Foundry Below and quest c8 — Implementation Plan

> Executed inline, as B-1 and B-2 were. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-act-iii-story-design.md` §3, beat 8. Task: WORK_PLAN B-3.

**Goal:** the Foundry Below is a Synth dungeon past Ironreach, open in the world and as private runs at every tier, with its reward chest. Quest c8 sends the player down it, which sets `foundry_below_cleared`.

## Decisions (defaults; flag them for the user)
1. **No G-2 twist yet.** B-3 depends on G-2 (a structural twist on the standard route), which isn't built. The Foundry Below uses the standard route now. G-2 can give it the twist later without touching the quest: the dungeon id and its entrance stay the same.
2. **Boss and mini-boss:** the boss is the Colossus, as *The Forge-Overseer*. The mini-boss is a Bulwark, *The Line Foreman*. Both are existing types. B-4's open-world Colossus gets named mechanics, which sets it apart.
3. **Sealed until the core is read:** `needs: { flag: 'titan_core_taken' }`. The core gave Isa the way down (c6 sets the flag; c8 comes after c7).
4. **Entrance:** a new story place, `foundrybelow`, *The Foundry Below*, out past Ironreach (`from: 'city3'`). It's a Synth blast hatch at the foot of a cut slope (`foundrybelow:hatch`, new model `hatch`). It's entered like the Brood Pits' mouth, and from every city's waygate once the flag is set.
5. **Level 21, `open: true`.** It joins LAUNCH (dungeoncheck's runs), but not "Into Every Dark", whose text names the first three. It gets a dungeon achievement of its own: *Down the Line*.

## Checks first
- **questcheck:**
  - The `foundrybelow` place stands within reach of Ironreach, with its hatch.
  - The playthrough sets `foundry_below_cleared` and completes c8 on every choice path.
  - c8's dungeon step points at its entrance.
- **dungeoncheck:**
  - The Foundry Below is refused at its hatch without `titan_core_taken`, and let in with it.
  - Its layout is checked across private runs (automatic, as for every dungeon).
- **poicheck and buildcheck:** the new place builds, and nothing earlier moves.
- **progresscheck:** the new achievement counts a Foundry Below clear.
